Privacy Policy

Shorts Uploader · Effective October 31, 2026

1. Information We Access

When the operator signs in with their own Google account, Shorts Uploader requests only these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the operator's channel
  • youtube.readonly: to check the processing and privacy status of uploaded videos

Shorts Uploader receives an OAuth token issued by Google. It does not collect any other personal information such as name, email address or contacts.

2. How We Use It

The scopes and token are used only to upload videos approved by the operator to the operator's own YouTube channel, schedule their publication, and check upload results.

3. Storage and Retention

The token is stored encrypted on the operator's computer and is never sent to any external server. It is deleted when the operator disconnects the account or stops using the tool.

4. Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or any other purpose. Shorts Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Shorts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of personal data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking Access

You can revoke Shorts Uploader's access at any time from Google Account > Security > Third-party access.
